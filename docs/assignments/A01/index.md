Clothes Pin
Patent: US2471606A
a.
The clothespin converts a force applied at its two jaws into a clamping force between them, allowing it to restrain a flat or flexible object to itself or to another object, such as a clothesline.

b.
The governing model for this product is its moment of equilibrium about its pivot axis.
 ∑M=0
 For the spring, this equation can be used: Ms​ = k_θ ​θ, where Ms is the spring restoring torque, k_θ is the torsional spring constant, and θ is the spring's angular displacement from an unloaded position.
 The jaw-closing force produced by the spring's torque can be shown as: Fjaw ​= Ms/r, where r is the perpendicular distance from the pivot to where the jaws connect.
 Assumption:
 Treat the two wooden jaws as rigid bodies and assume that the spring remains within its elastic range. This way, the torque is proportional to the angular displacement, giving us a proper mechanical model.​
